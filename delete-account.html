Delete Your Account

You can request permanent deletion of your TOEFL WIN account and the personal data associated with it at any time.

How to request deletion

Send an email to: wineexamapp@gmail.com
Subject: Delete My Account

Please include the following information in your request:

  • The email address used to create your TOEFL WIN account.
  • A clear statement that you want your account permanently deleted.

What will be deleted

  • Your authentication account.
  • Your saved profile information.
  • Your premium-status records stored by TOEFL WIN.
  • Your stored exam progress and related app data, where applicable.

Processing time

We aim to process valid deletion requests within 7 days. We may contact you to verify account ownership before completing the request.

Certain transaction, billing, fraud-prevention, security, or legal records may be retained only when required by law or by the services used to process purchases. Such records are not used for advertising.
