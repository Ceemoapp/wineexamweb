This Privacy Policy explains how WineExam (“WineExam,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the WineExam mobile applications, website, and related services (collectively, the “Service”).

We may process information generated while you use the Service, including selected answers, test progress, test history, scores, saved progress, mistake-book entries, written responses, speaking responses, transcripts, AI feedback, and usage-limit data.

1.3 Microphone and Audio Data

The Speaking features may request access to your device microphone so that you can record an answer. Microphone access is used only when you choose to use a speaking or recording feature.

Premium subscriptions and in-app purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store.   • provide, operate, maintain, and secure the Service;

When you request an AI evaluation, relevant content such as your written response, question text, transcript, audio-derived content, and technical request data may be sent to an AI service provider solely to generate the requested result and maintain the security of the service.

AI-generated scores, corrections, explanations, and feedback are automated outputs. They may be incomplete, inconsistent, or inaccurate and should not be treated as an official TOEFL score or as a decision made by ETS or any examination authority.

Deleting the app from your device does not automatically delete account information held by remote service providers.
